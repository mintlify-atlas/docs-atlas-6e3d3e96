SoftwareCopyright-Skill is a Codex Skill that automates the most tedious part of Chinese software copyright (软件著作权) registration: preparing the application materials. Install it once, point it at your project, and it guides you through every confirmation step before producing a ready-to-submit package of Word documents and TXT files — all generated locally from your real source code.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the skill">
    Copy `software-copyright-materials/` into your Codex skills directory and restart Codex.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your project in Codex">
    Navigate to the project you want to register, then ask Codex to generate 软著申请资料.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm at each stage">
    The skill pauses at seven mandatory gates — business understanding, field values, code selection, and more — so you stay in control of every decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your output package">
    After your final confirmation, the skill writes `软件著作权申请资料/正式资料/` containing a `.docx` operation manual, front/back 30-page code documents, and a `申请表信息.txt` ready for copy-paste into the official filing system.
  </Step>
</Steps>
